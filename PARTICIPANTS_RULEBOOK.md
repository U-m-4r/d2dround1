# 🕵️‍♂️ Decode2Deploy — Official Challenge Rules

Welcome to Decode2Deploy. Please read this brief guide to understand the game mechanics, allowed tools, and anti-cheat policies.

---

## 🎮 1. Gameplay & Scoring
* **5 Encrypted Levels**: Solve clues sequentially. Completing one level automatically unlocks the next.
* **Solves Matter Most**: Teams are ranked primarily by the total number of clues solved.
* **Single System Submissions**: It is highly recommended that each team submits answers using a **single primary system (one laptop/computer)**. Logging in or submitting from multiple systems simultaneously can cause session conflicts, override your teammates' progress, or trigger rate-limit blocks twice as fast.
* **Millisecond Precision**: If team scores are tied, the winner is determined by the **earliest timestamp** of the final level solve (tracked down to the millisecond).

---

## 🟢 2. What is ALLOWED (Do's)
* **Browser DevTools**: Opening Inspect Element (F12), checking console logs, and network background signals.
* **Decoding Tools**: Using hex, binary, or base64 decoders (such as [CyberChef](https://cyberchef.io/)).
* **Team Collaboration**: Teammates working together, sharing screens, and pair-programming.

---

## 🔴 3. What is PROHIBITED (Don'ts)
* **Cross-Team Sharing**: Sharing clues, hints, solutions, or portal credentials with other teams (leads to **instant disqualification**).
* **Automated Scripts**: Writing bots to brute-force answers or spam guesses.
* **Server Attacks**: Attempting to hack, crash, or overload the platform servers.

---

## 🚨 4. Anti-Cheat & Guess Limits
* **Guesser Block (Rate Limit)**: You are allowed a maximum of **2 guess attempts every 30 seconds**. Guessing faster will temporarily lock your screen.
* **Suspicious Activity Flag**: Submitting **more than 10 wrong answers within a 5-minute window** automatically flags your team as suspicious in red on the admin dashboard for manual moderator review.
